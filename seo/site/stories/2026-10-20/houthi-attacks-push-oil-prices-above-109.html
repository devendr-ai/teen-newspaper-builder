Houthi Attacks Push Oil Prices Above $109

Saudi Arabia has put off a big meeting of business leaders.

The Houthis are a group from Yemen. Yemen is the country just south of Saudi Arabia. They have kept attacking Saudi Arabia. On October 10, a strike hit the main airport in Riyadh, the Saudi capital. Saudi Arabia says at least 12 people were killed and more than 300 were hurt.

The attacks are now shaking the oil market. Saudi Arabia sells a lot of oil. After the Houthis claimed new attacks on Saudi airports and oil company sites, the price of Brent crude rose above $109 a barrel. Brent is a price that traders use for oil. UK ship monitors also said two tankers were attacked off the Saudi coast on Friday.

Life in Riyadh is changing too. A tall office tower there, the Kingdom Centre, told workers to stay home and work from there. And on Monday, Saudi Arabia put off a big investor meeting to 2027. It was due to start on October 26.

Why it matters

Saudi oil is sold around the world. When it is at risk, fuel prices can rise far from the fighting.
